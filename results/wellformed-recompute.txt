EFFECT ON REPORTED COVERAGE
  The paper reports coverage on the 26-document corpus as a mean over three runs
  (294.7 ± 5.2 of 572 slots). The archived harvest is a single run, so the exact
  corrected mean cannot be derived from it; what is measured here is that the check
  withholds 7 of 281 surfaced values in that harvest. Applying the same
  proportion to the reported mean gives approximately 287.4 of 572, a reduction of
  about 7.3 slots. This is an ESTIMATE, flagged as such: the campaign should be
  re-run before the corrected figure is printed as a measurement.

  Note the direction of the correction. Coverage falls slightly and no accuracy
  figure improves, because the withheld values were wrong to begin with. The paper
  gains a defect that its own audit caught, not a better number.
